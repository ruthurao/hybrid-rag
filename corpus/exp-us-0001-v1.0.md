## EXP-1.1 Purpose

This procedure (exp-us-0001, v1.0) governs the submission, review, and reimbursement of employee business expenses. Amounts stated in this procedure are in USD. This procedure applies to all employees seeking reimbursement for business-related expenses incurred in the course of their duties.

## EXP-2.1 Definitions

Reimbursable expense means a business-related cost incurred by an employee that is eligible for repayment under this procedure. Receipt means an itemized proof of purchase showing the vendor, date, and amount paid. Meal expense means the cost of food and non-alcoholic beverages incurred by an employee in connection with business activity, whether taken alone or with others. Submission date means the date an employee formally submits an expense claim for review.

## EXP-3.1 Submission requirements

An employee shall submit an expense claim through the expense system, itemizing each expense by date, amount, and business purpose. A claim missing an itemized business purpose is returned to the employee for correction before it proceeds to review.

## EXP-3.2 Receipt requirements

An employee shall attach a receipt for any single expense exceeding $25. An expense of $25 or less may be submitted without a receipt, provided the amount and business purpose are recorded in the claim.

## EXP-4.1 Meal expense caps

A meal expense of $75 or less per meal is reimbursable upon submission of a valid claim and, where required under Section EXP-3.2, a receipt. This cap applies per individual meal, not to an employee's total daily meal spend.

## EXP-4.2 Non-reimbursable items

Alcohol is not reimbursable under this procedure. Where a meal receipt includes alcohol as a line item, the employee shall deduct the cost of the alcohol from the claimed amount before submission. A claim found to include the cost of alcohol is returned to the employee for correction.

## EXP-4.3 Exceeding the meal cap

A meal expense exceeding $75 requires the approval of the employee's supervisor before reimbursement. The employee shall note the reason for the excess in the claim at submission. Where supervisor approval is not obtained, the claim is capped at $75 and the remaining amount is not reimbursed.

## EXP-5.1 Submission timing

An employee shall submit an expense claim within 30 days of the date the expense was incurred. A claim submitted after this period requires the approval of the employee's supervisor before it is processed.

## EXP-6.1 Exception and escalation

Where a claim is submitted without a required receipt, exceeds the meal cap without supervisor approval, is submitted after the 30-day period without supervisor approval, or is found to include alcohol, the claim is returned to the employee or referred to the supervisor for review. The supervisor may approve the claim as submitted, approve it with adjustment, or decline reimbursement of the disputed portion. A claim referred to the supervisor is not processed for payment until the supervisor's decision is recorded.

## EXP-7.1 Recording and outcome

The outcome of each claim, including the amount submitted, the amount approved, and any supervisor approval obtained, is recorded against the original claim record. A record of any deduction for alcohol, any excess meal expense approved, and any late submission approved is retained with the claim for audit purposes.
